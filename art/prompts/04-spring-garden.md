## Prompt für den trockenen Garten

Create one new wide landscape 16:9 background for the existing comic adventure Tamatem & Dina, using the attached references for the identical character and warm ink/hatching comic style. NO text, NO captions, NO UI. Scene: the DRY SPRING GARDEN beneath a dark cliff at Glass Mountain at twilight. Foreground left: Tamatem, visibly a SMALL SIX YEAR OLD boy, platinum blond hair, brown eyebrows and brown eyes, fair skin, beige field jacket over petrol sweater vest, brown shorts and small travel backpack, with Oris a barn owl perched nearby. Centre-right: an imposing but thoughtful nonhuman guardian made of dark volcanic rock and exposed roots, faint golden inner light, protecting a CLOSED stone archway in the cliff into a flower chamber. No Dina in this scene. The garden plants are wilted and dull olive, their leaves hanging down, dry roots round a shallow empty root basin. A small clear spring trickles out of the cliff into a tiny pool on the left but NOT into the dry garden. An ancient stone water mechanism is visible on the ground between the spring and the root basin, without letters or diagrammatic arrows. This is a narrative illustration; an exact six-tile channel puzzle is separately rendered in HTML, so do NOT bake an exact solved puzzle into this image. Emphasize: spring available, garden dry, guardian blocking the closed chamber. Clear readable silhouettes, dark petrol shadows, subtle golden highlights, hand-inked contours and fine crosshatching, same European adventure comic style as reference. Keep most of the environment visible, no closeup. No modern electronics, no neon, no written words. Produce a single illustration.

## Bearbeitungsprompt für den bewässerten Garten

Als Bildreferenz wurde der erzeugte trockene Garten verwendet.

Edit this exact comic game background to create its WATERED/RESTORED state. Preserve the exact camera, composition, architecture, boy's pose and identity, barn owl, and all object locations. Keep identical hand-inked European adventure comic linework and hatching. The boy remains a small six-year-old with platinum blond hair, brown eyebrows, brown eyes, beige field jacket and backpack. Change the dead wilted plants to lush revived green ferns, upright leaves and small subtle golden glowing flowers, filling the dry foreground root basin with clear blue water. Water now runs in the existing stone channels from the left spring into that root basin. The garden's light feels warmer, hopeful and golden; retain twilight sky. The imposing stone-and-root guardian has moved his blocking arm aside, looking welcoming rather than threatening. The stone double door behind him is OPEN into a softly golden-lit chamber; do not show the interior plant yet. No lettering, no captions, no UI. This is a state variation of the same exact place, not a different location. Do not add other people. Do not change the image dimensions.

## Anwendung
